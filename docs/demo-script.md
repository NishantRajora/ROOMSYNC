# Five-minute demo

1. Open the dashboard and show the compatibility feed. Point out that scores include human-readable reasons and conflicts.
2. Open a listing and explain trust signals: price, scam language, owner verification, and completeness.
3. Open the safety map and explain the sample risk components and NCU commute band.
4. Upload a sample agreement and show highlighted risky clauses, suggestions, and the informational-only disclaimer.
5. Finalise the document in local chain mode, show its SHA-256 hash, then verify the original and a modified copy.
